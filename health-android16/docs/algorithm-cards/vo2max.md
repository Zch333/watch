# Vendor VO2max 1.0.0

- Intended use: trend of platform-computed VO2max values.
- Inputs: qualified `VO2MAX_VENDOR` in ml/kg/min and vendor provenance.
- Method/output: median ml/kg/min for the selected period.
- Quality gate: approved catalog and unit/type semantics; rejected values excluded.
- Evidence: E0 vendor-unverified; target-device and activity eligibility validation pending.
- Failure modes: changing vendor model, unsuitable workouts, profile/context errors and sparse updates.
- License: proprietary application aggregation; vendor terms must be reviewed.
- Prohibited claims: clinical cardiorespiratory fitness diagnosis or exercise clearance.
