# SpO2 1.0.0

- Intended use: descriptive median/minimum and count of qualified samples below 90%.
- Inputs: platform SpO2 observations, 0–100%, with measurement context/provenance.
- Method/output: median/minimum percent and a sample count, not duration.
- Quality gate: rejected or invalid values excluded; no interpolation.
- Evidence: E1 engineering; device/skin/perfusion/motion validation pending.
- Failure modes: loose fit, cold skin, motion, low perfusion, sparse spot checks.
- License: proprietary application code; descriptive statistics unrestricted.
- Prohibited claims: hypoxemia, infection or sleep-apnoea diagnosis; the count is not an alarm.
