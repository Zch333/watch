# Vendor stress 1.0.0

- Intended use: show distribution/trend of Huawei-computed stress scores.
- Inputs: qualified `STRESS_VENDOR` values and Huawei algorithm provenance.
- Method/output: arithmetic mean and maximum in vendor score units.
- Quality gate: semantic range/catalog mapping must be confirmed; rejected values excluded.
- Evidence: E0 vendor-unverified until Huawei semantics and target-device behavior are documented.
- Failure modes: opaque vendor changes, sparse wear, context and firmware differences.
- License: proprietary application aggregation; Huawei score ownership/terms must be reviewed.
- Prohibited claims: psychological disorder, cortisol or medical stress diagnosis.
